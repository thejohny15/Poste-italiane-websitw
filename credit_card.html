Data source: FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data)

How these measures are built:
  • Debt: revolving consumer credit (REVOLSL).
  • Leverage ratios: debt divided by GDP (GDP) and disposable income (DPI), shown in %.
  • Cost of credit: APR (TERMCBCCALLNS), Fed Funds (FEDFUNDS), and spread = APR − Fed Funds.
  • Stress: delinquency (DRCCLACBS) and net charge-off (CORCCACBS) rates.
  • Frequency alignment: lower-frequency series are forward-filled to compare on a common monthly timeline.
