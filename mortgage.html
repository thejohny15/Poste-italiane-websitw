Data source: FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data)

Series in this chart:
  • 30Y Fixed: MORTGAGE30US.
  • 15Y Fixed: MORTGAGE15US.
  • 5/1 ARM: MORTGAGE5US.
  • House Price YoY: YoY % change from CSUSHPINSA.
  • Mortgage payment burden: MDSP (required mortgage payments as % of disposable personal income).
  • Mortgage distress: DRSFRMACBS (single-family mortgage delinquency rate at commercial banks).
  • Alternative broad pressure line: TDSP (total household debt service ratio).
  • Median household income growth: YoY-like growth from annual MEHOINUSA646N observations.
  • Labor market: UNRATE unemployment rate.
