Data source: Eurostat dataset isoc_e_dii

Methodology:
  • Population: Enterprises with size class selected on this page (size_emp=10-249 or size_emp=GE250).
  • Year: 2025 annual data.
  • Buckets: Very low, low, high, and very high digital intensity (DII Version 3).
  • Unit: Percentage of enterprises (PC_ENT).
  • Scope: All activities excluding agriculture, forestry, fishing, mining and quarrying, and without financial sector.
