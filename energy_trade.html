Data source: Eurostat dataset ext_st_eu27_2020sitc (table reference DS-059331)

Methodology:
  • Net balance: stk_flow=BAL_RT, indic_et=TRD_VAL_SCA, partner EXT_EU27_2020.
  • Oil section: SITC33.
  • Gases section: derived as total energy SITC3 minus oil SITC33.
  • Frequency: Monthly observations aggregated to quarter sums.
