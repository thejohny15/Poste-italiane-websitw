Data sources: US Treasury Fiscal Data API, FRED (Federal Reserve), Yahoo Finance

How to read these measures:
  • Holdings (Billions USD): TIC country holdings of U.S. Treasury securities, merged from historical and recent TIC releases.
  • % of Total Debt: country holdings divided by total U.S. federal debt from FRED (GFDEBTN), aligned by month.
  • Net Sales / Valuation charts: changes are decomposed into transaction flow and valuation effects using TIC SLT fields.
  • Country labels: TIC is residency-based (holders located in each jurisdiction), not strictly sovereign-only ownership.
  • Belgium and UK interpretation: Belgium often reflects custody through Euroclear; the UK often reflects London’s role as a global trading/custody hub. This helps explain spikes and drops in UK Treasury holdings and can inflate the amount of Treasuries recorded under Belgium versus ultimate beneficial ownership.
