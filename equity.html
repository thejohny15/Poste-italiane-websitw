How to read these measures:
  • US Equity Holdings (Billions USD): TIC U.S. Corp. Equity position (for_lt_eqty_pos) held by foreign residents.
  • % chart: each country (and Euro Zone aggregate) divided by a CRSP US Total Market Index-based denominator (index scaled to the latest total foreign equity level).
  • S&P 500 % chart: each country (and Euro Zone aggregate) divided by an S&P 500 market-cap proxy (latest total cap anchor scaled by monthly S&P 500 index levels).
  • Euro component % chart: each Euro country shown as its share of total Euro Zone U.S. corporate equity for each month.
  • Coverage: this SLT equity series starts in 2020 in the current TIC release format.
  • Interpretation: country values are residency-based and may include custodial/intermediary locations.
